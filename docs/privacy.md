# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Beta Social ("the app") is a desktop tool operated by Beta Hardware, İskenderun, Türkiye ("we"). The app is used only to manage Beta Hardware's own Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

## Data the app accesses

When the account owner connects an account and grants permission, the app accesses, through the official TikTok and Instagram APIs:

- **Profile information:** account ID, display name, username, avatar.
- **Account statistics:** follower count, following count, total likes, number of videos.
- **Content metrics** for the account's own posts and videos: views, likes, comments, shares, saves, reach, and watch-time metrics where available.
- **Videos and captions** that the account owner chooses to publish through the app.

The app does not access direct messages, contact lists, or other users' private data.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to:

- produce daily performance reports for the account owner;
- publish content that the account owner has reviewed and approved;
- keep the connection active by refreshing access tokens.

## Where the data is stored

- All data is stored **locally** on the account owner's computer in a SQLite database file.
- Access tokens are stored in the operating system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager).
- We do not operate a server that receives this data, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok and Instagram (Meta) when the app calls their APIs.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the account owner deletes it. The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the local database file and removing the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or from Instagram's settings for connected apps.

## Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.
